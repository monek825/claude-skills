# Voice, production and delivery format

## Writing for the ear
- **Spell out what must be spoken:** "dua ratus lima puluh gram", "tiga detik". Digits get mispronounced.
- **Write words the way they are said:**
  - "hape", not HP (which TTS reads as "haitch pee")
  - "Brek" for Breq
  - "gugling"
  - "cekot" if "checkout" sounds wrong
  - "wotsap" if "WhatsApp" fails
- **Speaking pace:** about 2.3–2.8 words per second for calm delivery, in Indonesian or English. A 40 s video holds about 90–110 words, fewer if there are pauses. Check with a timer.
- **Write the pause in** where a joke or reveal lands:
  - `[pause]` for a short one
  - `[long pause]` for about 1 s
  - "..." if the TTS ignores the tags
  In the edit, hold the picture 1–1.5 s on those beats.

## ElevenLabs v4 tags
- **Use tone tags only where the tone changes:** [sighs], [deadpan], [flat], [whispering], [warm], [dry], [casual], [matter-of-fact], [reverent], [beat], [pause], [long pause].
- **At most two tags in a row.** If a tag gets read out loud, move it before the first word or drop it.
- **Settings that worked (Oct 2026):** stability about 35–45% (lower means more emotion and more mistakes), similarity about 70–80%, and the language set explicitly.
- **Takes:** generate 4–5 takes of the whole block and keep the best. Then regenerate only the weak lines and splice them in at the pauses.
- **Language:** Indonesian for local buyers, because trust and humour land in their own language. English for global brand pieces.
- **Voice:** a calm male voice suits dry comedy; a female UGC-style voice suits testimonial or review formats.

## Beat sheet format (always deliver this)
| Time | Voiceover | Visual | Text overlay | Lens and light | SFX | Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:
- **Change something visual every 2–5 s:** a cut, a push-in, text animating in, an object entering.
- **Pacing:** comedy beats use fast cuts (about 12 frames) and a still camera for deadpan. Product and reveal beats use slow moves (3–5 s).
- **Direction and speed for every move:** "slides in from the right, 0.4 s".
- **Mark each shot's source:** filmed, screen-recorded or AI-generated. Never let AI generate text, UI, prices or logos.
- **Music cues:** where it starts, peaks and **hard-stops**. A dead stop on the punchline beats a fade.
- **One look:** use the same grain and grade on every shot, so mixed sources look like one camera.

## Captions and on-screen text
- **Burned-in subtitles for every spoken line,** because most viewers start on mute. About 54 px, in the lower third, at least 260 px above the bottom edge (the Reels UI).
- **The text hook** in the first 3 s may differ from the subtitle. Keep it under two lines.

## Post caption
- **The first line is a second hook** (a question or a test), not a summary.
- **One action at the end** ("Tulis angkanya di komentar", "DM 'DEMO'").
- **3–5 specific hashtags,** not a wall of them.
- **Label concept work** ("Contoh memakai brand fiktif").

## Checking after posting
- **Retention at 3 s** judges the hook.
- **Average watch time and percentage** judge the whole script.
- **Sends per reach** measures the "send it to a friend" factor.

Fix the moment the retention curve drops in the next script.
