# Worked examples (passed review)

The full versions live in the Breq repo: `~/Documents/Website Business/HQ/content/LAUNCH-VIDEO-D.md` and `VALUE-VIDEOS.md`.

## 1. Launch reel D, "Toko sebelah" (the bad-news spine, about 52 s, Indonesian male voice)
> [sighs] Ada kabar buruk. [deadpan] Di Shopee, produk kamu dipajang persis di sebelah puluhan toko yang jual barang sama. [long pause] Yang lebih murah. [flat] Di Instagram, link bio kamu langsung lempar ke WhatsApp, tanpa satu pun foto produk. [long pause] [dry] Terus kamu heran kenapa orang nanya, "ini asli nggak, Kak?" [pause] [warm] Website itu etalase kamu sendiri. Di sana, toko sebelah nggak ikut nongol. [matter-of-fact] Di Brek Studio, kami bikin website yang kelihatan semahal produk kamu. Produk kamu muncul di layar pertama, dan tombol pesannya langsung ke WhatsApp kamu. [casual] Sebelum kamu bayar apa pun, kami bikinin dulu contoh tampilannya. Gratis. [dry] Tapi kalau makanannya nggak enak, website nggak bakal nolong. [long pause] [warm] Kalau enak... [beat] jangan dijual di sebelah toko yang lebih murah.

Why it works:
- The hook is three words, followed by a problem the viewer sees every day.
- "Yang lebih murah." on its own after a long pause is the sting.
- It uses the buyer's own words ("ini asli nggak, Kak?").
- The heaven shot turns the argument into a picture: the competitors dissolve into clouds.
- The honest twist builds trust.
- The ratio is about 2:1 problem to brand, and the free demo removes the risk.

Rejected earlier drafts, and why:
- "Satu halaman. Produk paling depan. Tombol pesan yang langsung kelihatan.": a three-fragment list.
- A 190-word version: too long, and the jokes had no room.
- "HP" in the voiceover: the TTS read it as "haitch pee".

## 2. Value-proposition reel (about 40 s)
> [deadpan] Ini yang kamu dapat kalau bikin website di Brek. [pause] Tombol pesan yang udah ngetik pesanannya sendiri. [beat] "Halo, saya mau pesan keripik balado, dua ratus lima puluh gram." [casual] Pembeli tinggal kirim. [pause] [flat] Link yang muncul lengkap dengan foto produk waktu dibagikan di grup arisan. [pause] Nomor halal yang bisa dicek pembeli sendiri, sekali klik. [pause] QR di kemasan. [beat] Jadi tiap bungkus yang habis dimakan, bisa langsung dipesan lagi. [dry] Websitenya atas nama kamu. Bukan sewaan. [long pause] Omzet? Itu urusan rasa keripikmu. [warm] Bagian kami, pembeli yang mampir langsung tahu harus pesan di mana.

Why it works:
- Each value is a moment the seller recognises (the arisan group, the empty packet), not a feature name.
- The ending says what the business controls, and hands sales back with a joke.

## 3. SteelSeries Sensei Pro / Rival Pro (reference, not our work)
The transcript was read from the burned-in subtitles. The structure is in `structures.md` §3.

Its signature moves:
- deadpan narration over cinematic product shots
- a Creation-of-Adam "heaven" parody played straight
- a callout to a weird subgroup ("claw grip weirdos")
- a personal jab ("actually, it's you Jon")
- a self-deprecating truth ("if you suck you'll still suck")
- a two-word end tag

Copy the grammar, never the lines.
