# Reel scripts

A short video is judged in three numbers: how many people stay past the first 3 seconds, how much of it they watch (percentage and seconds), and how many send it to someone. Instagram's head (Mosseri, Jan 2025) named **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** as the top Reels signals, and sends matter most for reaching people who do not follow you. Every rule below serves one of those three.

Read before writing:
- `references/hooks.md`: hook formulas, the 4 ingredients, text/spoken/visual alignment, failure modes
- `references/structures.md`: script templates with timings
- `references/anti-slop.md`: banned patterns and the rewrite rules (run it on every draft)
- `references/voice-and-production.md`: writing for the ear, ElevenLabs tags, Indonesian notes, beat-sheet format, captions
- `references/examples.md`: annotated scripts that passed review (Breq launch reel D, value-proposition reel, SteelSeries breakdown)
- `references/research-notes.md`: what the research says, how strong each claim is, sources

## The process (do it in this order)

1. **Pin the angle.** Write one sentence: *who* is watching, *what they believe now*, *what they should believe after*. If the request is vague ("a video about our websites"), offer 3 angles first: a villain, a mechanism and a mistake (e.g. "the bio link that sends buyers to an empty WhatsApp", "how a pre-filled order message works", "stop putting your halal logo inside an image"), and let the user pick.
2. **Find the mechanism.** A script without a "how it works" is an ad full of claims. Name the concrete mechanism in one line (the message types itself; the link card shows the product photo; the QR on the packet opens the order page).
3. **Write 3–5 hooks, pick one.** Use `hooks.md`. Each must say what the video is about AND why to keep watching, in under 15 spoken words, with matching on-screen text and a first frame that moves.
4. **Pick a structure** from `structures.md` that fits the goal (top of funnel, value, offer, comedy). Default length: 20–45 s for a value video, 40–60 s for a launch or story reel. Cut anything that does not raise curiosity, deliver value or set up the payoff.
5. **Draft the voiceover for the ear** (`voice-and-production.md`): one idea per sentence, spoken words, numbers spelled out, pauses written in where a joke or reveal needs to land.
6. **Build the beat sheet**: for every line, time / voiceover / on-screen visual / text overlay / lens and light / SFX / music. Change something visual every 2–5 s. The story must still read with the sound off.
7. **Run the review gate** (below). Fix, then re-run.
8. **Deliver**: the voiceover as one paste-ready block, the beat sheet, 2 alternative hooks with one-line reasons, a caption whose first line is a second hook, 3–5 specific hashtags, and a "confirm before posting" list of every fact the user must verify.

## Non-negotiables

- **Facts:** no invented numbers, results, testimonials, client names or statistics. If a claim needs proof the user has not given, cut it or flag it in "confirm before posting".
- **No promises you can't keep:** never promise sales, rankings, virality or "appear in ChatGPT". Promise what the business controls.
- **Real brands on screen only with written permission.** Use invented brands for demos and label concept work.
- **Platform UI and logos:** do not reproduce another company's interface or logo as if real; use generic look-alikes. AI video must never draw text, prices or UI; add those in the edit or by screen recording.
- **One video, one idea.** Two ideas means two videos.
- **Payoff before CTA.** Never end on a CTA alone; the viewer must get the punchline, result or answer first.

## Review gate (score every draft; anything under 4 gets rewritten)

| Check | Pass when |
|---|---|
| Hook clarity | A stranger knows the topic and why to stay within 3 s, sound off |
| Alignment | Spoken hook, on-screen text and first frame all say the same thing |
| Mechanism | The video shows *how*, not only *that* |
| Value | The viewer gets something useful even if they never buy |
| Rhythm | Sentence lengths vary; jokes and reveals have written-in pauses; visual change every 2–5 s |
| Slop | Zero hits from `anti-slop.md` |
| Truth | Every fact is sourced or flagged; no promised outcomes |
| Ending | Real payoff, then (optional) one clear CTA; the last frame can loop into the first |
| Sendability | There is a line or moment someone would send to a friend ("this is literally you") |

Then read the voiceover out loud at speaking pace with a timer. If it overruns the target length by more than 10%, cut words, not pauses.
