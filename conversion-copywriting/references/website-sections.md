# Plan individual sections

Read for section-level work. These are practical defaults to adapt, not universally winning arrangements. Read website-types.md for industry fit. Respect the project's existing design rules and approved references before applying patterns.

For every section decide: visitor question → verified answer → useful presentation → next action. Omit sections without a job or usable content. A targeted section edit does not require rewriting the entire page.

## Navigation

Use task-recognizable labels and working destinations. Separate existing-customer tasks such as tracking/support from new-business enquiries. Retain useful research routes on multipurpose sites. Test the mobile menu, close behavior and destination. Navigation breadth follows the actual information inventory, not a prescribed link count.

## Hero

Establish what the business offers, who/where it serves when material, why the offer is credible, and a predictable next step. Use concrete copy and an asset that explains the offer. Choose composition from audience/task/assets/reference: real product image, actual interface, real project/venue, direct text, or task/catalog entry. Split layouts, centered type, and full-screen images are options, not rules.

Keep category/offer clear even with an evocative brand headline. Supporting copy can explain scope or mechanism; relevant evidence can substantiate it. Do not manufacture proof to fill a hero. Verify mobile crop, text readability, ordering and load behavior. Background video is not needed simply to look premium.

CTA labels should describe the destination: view menu, explore products, request quote, see workflow. Free/demo/availability wording must match actual behavior and terms. Do not hide an enquiry behind language suggesting immediate booking. Comprehension checks diagnose clarity, not commercial lift. [NN/g homepage principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/).

## Products/services and value

Group by buyer selection criteria; each item should identify, distinguish and explain its destination. Give physical products real usage/detail views, software real workflow/screen evidence, services actual deliverables/work. Use paragraph explanation, parallel facts, and deeper specifications when needed. Avoid unnecessary filters for a tiny range.

Connect benefits to actual mechanisms and relevant situations. Replace generic quality/innovation/excellence claims with useful specific facts. An attractive truck photograph cannot substantiate a cold-chain capability. A mock interface cannot substantiate an integration. Check images and claims against the same actual offer.

## Proof

Match proof to uncertainty: real work for visual quality; specs/workflow for fit; real identity/contact for accountability; process for implementation; attributable case studies for result claims. Place proof near the relevant decision. Logos require permission and a truthful relationship; numbers require definition/timeframe; certifications require accurate scope. No evidence available → use actual work and clear facts, or record a gap outside publishable copy. Do not generate testimonials or claims.

## Pricing

Match presentation to the model:

- Fixed item/menu: price, unit/portion, variants and material fees/conditions.
- Package: deliverables, exclusions, scope conditions, payment and next step.
- Subscription: billing interval, actual commitment, allowances, overages, cancellation and meaningful plan differences.
- Custom: verified ranges/examples when available, cost drivers and quote inputs.
- Enterprise: commercial scope and procurement path; don't invent a price to satisfy a template.

Starting prices need real achievable scopes and conditions. Monthly-equivalent annual prices need explicit annual billing/commitment. Recommended tiers should explain suitability; most-popular labels require real data. Three cards and a highlighted middle plan are conventions, not established optima.

Keep comparison labels/order consistent, material differences visible, and mobile plan/row context readable. Use representative scenarios for complex early-stage budgeting when verified; do not require a detailed calculator for rough shortlisting. [NN/g scenario pricing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/show-prices-for-common-scenarios/). Assess qualification/profit, not total leads alone.

## Process/about

Steps should explain a real sequence, client inputs, business responsibilities, milestones and supported timing/conditions. No invented response promises. About/team should establish relevant identity and accountability. Long origin stories should not obscure urgent buyer tasks. B2B content can support both users and approvers with operational and commercial details. [NN/g B2B differences](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-vs-b2c/).

## FAQ

Use real remaining questions. Put essential costs, scope, limits and eligibility in their main sections. Accordions suit selectively needed secondary answers; avoid hiding information everyone must compare or read in sequence. Make questions descriptive and controls accessible. [NN/g accordions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/accordions-on-desktop/).

## CTA/contact/footer

Match readiness: explore, inspect, enquire, reserve, or buy. Explain channel, needed information, and what happens next. Collect enough detail for the next useful action without speculative fields. Clear labels, suitable input types, retained values, meaningful errors and confirmation matter. [W3C labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/).

Check real link/form/chat destinations, delivery and confirmation. WhatsApp clicks, directions clicks, and demo-button clicks are intermediate events; establish downstream outcomes separately. Use existing reliable channels and respect scope. Footer needs actual identity/contact, useful destinations and applicable policies, not decorative or dead links.

## Review output

When asked for a section plan, provide purpose, content/proof, layout direction, action/destination, critical gaps and a proportionate check. Keep unsupported planning assumptions out of publishable copy. When auditing, distinguish observed defects from hypotheses and taste. Read experiments.md if proposing a measured test; never attach another company's uplift to a section recommendation.
