# Match structure to the business and visitor

Read only the relevant subtype. These are adaptable starting plans. Identify category, page job and visitor stage separately. Content facts and commercial terms must come from the brief or current verified business sources. Company pages below illustrate conventions; they do not establish causal conversion gains.

Respect scope: a showcase/enquiry site can link to an existing booking, store, trial, portal or tracking destination. Industry research does not authorize adding carts, accounts, calculators or backend functions.

## Product showcase

**Consumer product:** help visitors desire the product, evaluate fit, and find a buying destination. Start with the real product/use, then range/variants, relevant details and imagery, evidence, purchase/stockist routes and support. Choose scale, material, use and detail views that answer actual questions. Product imagery and readable detail complement one another. [Baymard product research](https://baymard.com/research/product-page) is ecommerce research; transfer cautiously to a non-cart showcase.

**Manufacturer/distributor:** support technical qualification and procurement. Start with capabilities/applications → categories → actual specifications and downloads → production/quality evidence → supply/commercial conditions → quote. Check units, limits, compatibility, minimums, regions and revision dates. Provide HTML essentials even when a technical sheet is available. A three-product range needs no elaborate filtering. Technical users and approvers may need different depth. [NN/g B2B differences](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-vs-b2c/).

**Success:** appropriate product enquiries/orders or existing-channel purchases, not gallery clicks alone.

## Food and beverage

**Restaurant/café:** help someone decide what to eat, whether/when to visit and how to get there. Show cuisine/venue identity with real photography, then bring current menu/prices and branch essentials forward: hours, address, directions, contact, booking/walk-in conditions. Order can vary with search/maps versus discovery traffic. Use a mobile-readable menu; don't rely on a zoom-only image/PDF. Confirm dietary/allergen facts with the accountable business source; never infer safety from photos or dish names. Atmosphere/story can follow useful visit information. [Cheesecake Factory](https://www.thecheesecakefactory.com/) illustrates separate menu, restaurant and order/reservation routes; a local café needs its own simpler inventory.

**Catering:** qualify occasion, date, headcount, area and service format. Hero/service fit → menus/packages → actual minimums, inclusions, price basis and lead-time conditions → logistics/process → real event proof → useful enquiry. Distinguish drop-off, staffed, venue-based and other services only when offered. Don't invent recurring ordering, staffing or delivery guarantees. Ask for date/location/quantity/contact when needed; detailed preferences can follow. [Anayat menu](https://www.anayatevent.com/menu) illustrates package scope and enquiry linkage, not universal pricing or terms.

**Packaged food brand:** show range and actual variants, usage, relevant approved ingredient/nutrition/allergen information, buying/stockist routes and consumer/distributor contact. Use current market-specific owner-approved data and packaging; do not infer claims from a partial list. Keep consumer imagery and useful information prominent. A wholesale visitor may additionally need case configuration, minimums and distribution terms. [Hershey allergen guidance](https://www.thehersheycompany.com/en_us/home/ingredients/allergens.html) illustrates checking current packaging because information can change.

**Success:** confirmed reservations/visits/orders or suitable catering/distribution enquiries; calls and directions clicks are intermediate signals.

## Logistics

**Carrier/parcel:** new shipment rates/booking, existing shipment tracking, and problem/support resolution are distinct tasks. Give recognizable separate destinations. Service/coverage → service comparison → rates/conditions or existing quote path → relevant proof → shipping requirements/support. Only use Ship now/Book when a real path exists. [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/home.html) illustrates separate rate, tracking and locations tasks.

**Freight forwarder/3PL:** demonstrate ability to move the visitor's actual cargo on the actual route. Scope/modes/regions → cargo and service constraints → process/handoffs/visibility → comparable proof → verified pricing examples or cost factors → quote. Useful initial details may include origin/destination, cargo, size/weight, timing/frequency and reply contact, depending on how the business quotes. Do not promise transit times, insurance, customs handling or temperature control without support. Keep existing tracking separate; link to the correct portal instead of inventing a tracker.

Publish stable information; explain variable pricing. An early buyer may need a verified representative cost rather than a demanding calculator; a quote-ready buyer may have detailed inputs. [NN/g scenario pricing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/show-prices-for-common-scenarios/) includes qualitative shipping-vendor observations, not randomized lift.

**Success:** serviceable quotes → accepted quotes/booked shipments/account value. Report tracking success separately from new-business conversion.

## Software

**Self-service SaaS:** real task/outcome → actual workflow/interface → benefits and proof → meaningful plans/limits → setup/integrations/support → existing trial/signup. Free account, time-limited trial, recorded demo and sales meeting are different offers. State card requirement, expiry behavior and conditions only when confirmed. Explain plan units, allowances, overages and billing commitments. A screen should demonstrate an actual capability, not decorate the page. [Slack free plan](https://slack.com/pricing/free) illustrates explicit limits; verify current facts rather than reuse its details.

**Enterprise software:** use case → workflow → supported integrations/architecture → relevant security evidence → comparable cases → implementation/support → commercial model → genuine demo/sales path. Let visitors independently evaluate essentials before contacting sales. Security logos require current scoped evidence; unknown certification means no claim. Users, technical reviewers, procurement and approvers can need different details. [Asana enterprise](https://asana.com/enterprise) illustrates multiple evaluation routes, not authority to offer a trial for another product.

**Software agency:** specialty/client fit → actual projects and role/results → engagement scope/deliverables → team/process → ownership/support conditions → budget drivers → project enquiry. Sell the actual engagement, not software plan tiers. Name technical capabilities only when supported. Broad goal, stage, timeframe and usable contact may suffice before detailed specifications. [thoughtbot enquiry](https://thoughtbot.com/hire-us) illustrates a project conversation route.

**Success:** SaaS activation/paid retention; enterprise qualified opportunities/contracts; agency suitable briefs/accepted profitable work. Demo clicks and signups alone are incomplete outcomes.

## Other common types

These are practical extensions of task-first planning; they are not individually proven industry conversion recipes.

| Type | Decision content and structure | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Local service/trade | Service and area → job types → actual work → scope/price factors → availability/contact | Suitable appointments/jobs |
| Architecture/interiors/construction | Specialty → relevant portfolio and project role → process/scope/team → project enquiry | Suitable briefs/commissions |
| Hospitality | Property/location → real rooms → relevant amenities/access → rates/conditions → existing reservation | Suitable completed bookings |
| Professional practice | Services/eligibility → relevant verified credentials → process/fees → appropriate contact | Suitable consultations; no fabricated outcomes |
| Nonprofit | Cause/action → real program → accountable evidence → giving options/terms → existing donation | Completed gifts/donor support |
| Ecommerce | Find item → variant/details → total cost/delivery/returns → purchase/checkout | Fulfilled profitable orders; needs authorized commerce scope |
| Editorial/education | Direct useful answer → readable context → credible sources/date → relevant next learning step | Agreed reader task, not arbitrary CTA pressure |

Observed patterns: [Snøhetta projects](https://www.snohetta.com/projects) organizes actual projects; [Marriott](https://www.marriott.com/default.mi) supports several travel tasks; [charity: water](https://www.charitywater.org/) combines cause, giving and accountability content. Public page text is not a responsive audit or proof of commercial performance.

## Apply without template filler

Give each proposed section a buyer question and real content. Move urgent information forward; omit unsupported sections rather than generate testimonials, stats, badges, timelines or prices. Preserve brand and approved references. Choose readable grouping/prose/bullets/tables from the content. If recommending tests, load experiments.md and evaluate downstream quality, not merely more clicks.
